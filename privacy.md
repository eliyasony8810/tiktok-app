# Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how this application ("the App") handles data. The App is a personal, non-commercial tool used only by the owner of the TikTok account @happy.soul888.

## What data the App accesses

With the owner's permission (through TikTok's official login), the App may read the following data from the owner's own TikTok account:

- basic profile information (display name, avatar);
- account statistics (followers, following, likes, video count);
- the list of the owner's own videos and their public statistics.

## How the data is used

The data is used only to help the owner analyze the performance of her own content.

## Sharing

The App does not sell, rent, share or transfer any data to third parties, and does not use the data for advertising.

## Storage and deletion

The App does not keep a permanent database of TikTok data. Access can be removed at any time in the TikTok app settings (Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Apps and services).

## Data of other users

The App does not collect data about other TikTok users.

## Changes

This policy may be updated at any time. The latest version is always published on this page.
